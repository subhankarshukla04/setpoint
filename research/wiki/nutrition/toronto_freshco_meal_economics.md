# Toronto / FreshCo Meal Economics

> Anchor a 200 g-protein day to bone-in chicken thighs, eggs, lentils and
> Costco whey; cross-shop FreshCo Bathurst, Bulk Barn and Costco to land a
> cutting day under $12 CAD even at 2026 inflation.

All prices CAD, **approximate, verify against current flyer**. Captured Apr–May 2026.

## What the evidence says

Canadian food inflation re-accelerated into 2026: StatsCan reports
food-from-stores +4.1% YoY in Feb 2026 after +4.8% in Jan 2026
[^statcancpi]. Dalhousie's *Canada's Food Price Report 2026* projects
+4–6% on the year, with meat the largest line and ~$994 added to the
family-of-four annual bill [^dalfpr2026]. FCC flags chicken demand strong
on beef substitution and Q4-2025 fresh/frozen chicken +6.7% — bone-in
cuts remain the best $/g protein vehicle [^fcc2026].

Frozen produce closes the nutrient gap and beats fresh on price. Bouzari
2015 and Li 2017 (UC Davis / UGA) measured eight fruit-and-veg pairs and
found vitamin C, β-carotene and folate **statistically equivalent or
higher** in frozen vs fresh-stored counterparts at five-day refrigeration
[^bouzari2015][^li2017]. Frozen broccoli, spinach, peppers and berries at
FreshCo run $2.49–4.99 per 500–600 g bag — typically 30–50% cheaper than
fresh and zero-waste [^natgeo].

The FreshCo at **410 Bathurst St** sits inside a dense cross-shop loop:
Bulk Barn (oats, lentils, spices), No Frills (dairy loss-leaders),
Kensington Market (produce, fish), Costco Downsview (whey, jasmine rice
8 kg, frozen chicken thighs, eggs by the flat) [^freshcoflyer]. FreshCo
wins on flyer protein and frozen veg; it loses on bulk dry goods,
supplements and high-turn dairy.

## Practical rules

- **Protein hierarchy ($/100 g protein, sale-typical):** dried lentils
  ~$1.55 → bone-in skin-on thighs ~$3.20–4.50 → whole chicken ~$3.00–4.70
  → Costco whey ~$2.80 → eggs ~$5–7 → Greek yogurt ~$6.65–8.65 → canned
  tuna ~$7–8 → cottage cheese ~$7.50–9.20.
- **FreshCo flyer trigger prices:**
  - Bone-in thighs ≤ **$3.99/lb** → buy 5–10 lb, freeze in portions.
  - Whole chicken ≤ **$2.99/lb** → roast + carcass stock.
  - Large eggs ≤ **$3.99/dozen** → 2–3 dozen.
  - Greek yogurt ≤ **$5/750 g** → 2 tubs.
- **Frozen-veg-first.** Default broccoli/spinach/pepper-onion/cauliflower
  to frozen Compliments bags. Cabbage and carrots stay the cheapest fresh
  fillers ($0.99–1.99/lb).
- **Cross-shop:** dry → Bulk Barn; whey + jasmine rice 8 kg + 4 kg frozen
  thighs + 24-flat eggs → Costco; loss-leader dairy → No Frills.
- **Skip at FreshCo:** whey ($1.50–2/serving vs Costco $0.85), in-shell
  almonds, any cut where Costco bone-in is < 60% the price.

## What this does not say

- Prices move weekly. Every figure is a point estimate ±20%; the rule is
  the *trigger threshold*, not the exact $.
- Ignores time cost of cross-shopping. A 90-min Costco run amortises
  across a month — single-week math underestimates Costco's edge.
- Frozen-equivalency findings cover vitamin C, β-carotene, folate, K. They
  do not extend to texture, polyphenols in soft fruit, or contexts where
  fresh is non-negotiable (salads, garnishes).
- Says nothing about adherence — see
  [satiety_high_volume_palatable_meals](../nutrition/satiety_high_volume_palatable_meals.md).
- Bulk-thigh purchases assume a freezer and ~1 hr/week of batch-cook.

## Worked example A — bulk day (2400 kcal / 200 g protein, ~$10.42 CAD)

Bone-in thighs 1 lb ($3.99) + 4 eggs ($1.33) + oats 100 g ($0.35) +
jasmine rice 200 g dry ($0.55) + frozen broccoli 250 g ($1.25) + 2%
cottage cheese 200 g ($1.80) + banana ($0.30) + 1 whey serving ($0.85).
Trim cottage cheese to 150 g and the day lands at **$9.97 / 2480 kcal /
200 g P**.

## Worked example B — cutting day (2000 kcal / 200 g protein, ~$10.51 CAD)

Whole chicken 0.75 lb cooked ($2.99) + 3 eggs ($1.00) + Greek yogurt 0%
300 g ($2.00) + dried lentils 80 g ($0.31) + russet potato 400 g ($0.53)
+ frozen spinach 200 g ($1.00) + frozen berries 150 g ($1.25) + 1.5 whey
servings ($1.28) + olive oil 10 g ($0.15). Adding a second whey serving
lifts protein to ≈ 211 g for **$11.55**.

Both days require at least one Costco staple. A pure-FreshCo build adds
roughly **+$1.50/day**.
